swordbot

swordbot is a personal customer-support assistant, run by its owner for their own use. The owner describes a problem with an online order in a Telegram chat. swordbot looks up the order's receipt in the owner's Gmail account, drafts an email to the merchant's customer support, and sends it from that account only after the owner approves that exact email by pressing Send.

It is not a public service: it only responds to its owner's Telegram account.
